# Code of conduct

This covers bpfrag's issues, pull requests, discussions and anywhere else
the project is being worked on.

## What's expected

- Be civil. Disagree about the measurement, the code or the decision, not
  about the person.
- Assume the other person is trying to help. Most people here are
  volunteering their time, the maintainer included.
- Back claims with something others can check: a timeline, a log, a
  source. "It feels faster" is a fine reason to open an issue and not a
  result.
- Accept "no" on scope. bpfrag is networking only, and some things are
  turned down for that reason alone.
- Keep other people's details out of it: no addresses, names or logs that
  identify someone who didn't choose to share them.

## What isn't acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or demeaning remarks about anyone, for any reason:
  who they are, where they're from, how much they know, what they play or
  what they run.
- Sexual language or imagery, and unwelcome advances.
- Threats, or publishing someone's private information.
- Persisting after being asked to stop.

## Reporting

Email <mail@shanefagan.com>. Reports are read by the maintainer and kept
private. Say what happened and where, with links if there are any.

## What happens

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, lock a thread, or block
someone from the project, for a while or for good, depending on what
happened and whether it's a pattern. A first, minor lapse usually gets a
private word. There's one maintainer, so there's no appeal beyond asking
him to look again.
